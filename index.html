ChaThai Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16

ChaThai is an offline Thai dictionary; its word list ships inside the app. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and we do not collect personal data.

Data on your device. Favorites, tags, review progress, custom words, search history and settings stay on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, they sync between your own devices through Apple's iCloud key-value storage; the developer cannot access them.

Camera and photos. Text recognition runs on your device. Photos are never uploaded.

Speech input. Uses Apple's speech recognition, on-device where possible.

Network. The app connects to the network only when you choose to download a language pack (from GitHub), and sends no personal data.

Contact: fire.university.tw@gmail.com

查泰隱私政策

最後更新：2026-11-16

查泰是離線泰語字典，詞庫內建在 App 裡。不要求註冊、不建立帳號，不收集你的個人資料，也不使用分析或廣告工具。

留在你裝置上的資料。收藏、標籤、複習進度、自訂單字、搜尋紀錄與設定都存在你的裝置上。若你登入 iCloud，這些資料會透過 Apple 的 iCloud 鍵值儲存在你自己的裝置之間同步；開發者看不到、也無法存取。

相機與相簿。文字辨識在裝置上進行，照片不會上傳。

語音輸入。使用 Apple 的語音辨識，優先在裝置上辨識。

網路連線。只有在你主動下載語言包時才會連線（從 GitHub 下載），不傳送任何個人資料。

聯絡：fire.university.tw@gmail.com
